Threat Information

Benign

Legitimate websites with no malicious content. Safe for browsing and user interaction without security concerns.

Phishing

Fraudulent sites designed to steal personal information and credentials by impersonating legitimate organizations.

Defacement

Websites that have been hacked and modified without authorization. May contain inappropriate content or redirect to malicious sites.

Malware

Sites distributing malicious software like viruses and ransomware. Can infect systems and compromise security.
